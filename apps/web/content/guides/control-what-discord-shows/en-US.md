Showing what you watch is fun until it isn't: a meeting, a late-night series binge, a playlist you would rather keep to yourself. Nowly gives you several levels of control, from a global pause to a per-presence privacy mode, and Discord adds its own settings on top. This guide goes from the broadest switch to the finest one.

## Discord's own settings come first

Whatever Nowly sends, Discord decides who sees it:

- **Activity Privacy** in Discord's **User Settings** turns activity sharing on or off for your whole account.
- Your **status** matters: when you are **Invisible**, nobody sees your activity.
- Some servers let you turn off activity sharing just for that server, from the server's privacy settings.

Your activity is visible to people who can see your profile: friends, and members of servers you share, depending on those settings. Keep that in mind before showing anything you would not say out loud in those servers.

## Pause everything in one shortcut

The fastest way to hide everything is to pause sharing:

- Press **Ctrl+Shift+U** (**Cmd+Shift+U** on a Mac) from any tab.
- Or click the pause button at the top of the Nowly side panel, or use **Pause sharing** in Settings.

While sharing is paused, nothing reaches Discord, the toolbar icon shows it, and the side panel says **Sharing is paused**. Your presences, settings and schedules stay as they are. Press the same shortcut to resume.

## Hide one tab

Sometimes only one tab is the problem. In the side panel's **Activity** tab, choose **Hide this tab**: Nowly stops sharing that tab and moves on to the next one that has something to show. **Share this tab** brings it back.

## Snooze a presence

To stop one platform for a while without uninstalling it, snooze its presence for **1 hour**, **4 hours** or **Until tomorrow**. The presence comes back on its own when the time is up. It is handy for a platform you use for work during the day and for fun at night.

## Share only during certain hours

Schedules decide when Nowly shares at all:

1. Open **Settings**, then **Schedule**, and turn on **Share on a schedule**.
2. Pick the days and the hours, for example weekday evenings and weekends.
3. Outside those hours, the side panel shows **Outside your schedule** and nothing reaches Discord.

Each presence can also have its own **Custom schedule** that replaces the global one, so you can share music all day but streaming only in the evening. Presences without their own schedule follow the global one.

## Use each presence's own options

Open a presence in the side panel to see its settings. They differ from one platform to another, because each one shows different things. A few examples from the library:

- **Browsing activity** is off by default on most presences. YouTube, Netflix, Twitch and others only share what you are actually watching unless you turn on **Show browsing activity**. A few, like Prime Video, Disney+ and Crunchyroll, also show the title page you are on.
- **Privacy mode** on Spotify, Deezer, Apple Music and SoundCloud hides the track title, artist, artwork and buttons. Discord then only shows that you are listening to music on that platform.
- **Privacy mode** on Crunchyroll hides the title you are watching. On Steam, it hides game, profile and page names.
- **ChatGPT** never shows the title of a temporary chat unless you turn on **Show temporary chats**, and **Show conversation title** can be turned off for every chat.
- **Show buttons** removes the buttons that let your friends open the same page, on presences that have them.
- **YouTube** only shows channel names and avatars on channel pages if you turn on **Show channel details**.

## Choose the language Discord shows

Presences can write their text on Discord in several languages. In **Settings**, **Discord language** sets it for all presences at once. Only the languages shared by all your installed presences are listed there, and you can pick a different language for a single presence from its own page. This changes what your friends read, not the language of the extension.

## Remove what you don't use

The simplest privacy setting is not to install a presence. A presence runs only on its own websites, and only after you install it. Remove the ones you no longer use from the side panel or from their library page, and Nowly leaves those websites alone.

## A quick routine

- Before a meeting or a stream: **Ctrl+Shift+U**.
- For a platform you want to keep private: its privacy mode, or no presence at all.
- For a predictable rhythm: a schedule, with custom hours for the presences that need them.
