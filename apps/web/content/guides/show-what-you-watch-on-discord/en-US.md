Discord shows the game you play out of the box, but not the series you watch in your browser. With Nowly, your profile can say **Watching Netflix** with the title, the episode, the poster and how far you are, and your friends can open the same episode with one click. This guide explains how to set it up for streaming services and what to expect from each of them.

## What you need

If Nowly is not set up yet, follow [How to set up Nowly](/guides/set-up-nowly) first: the extension, user scripts, the desktop app and the Discord desktop app. Then install a presence for each service you use from the [library](/library). Streaming presences include Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Crunchyroll, HBO Max, Paramount+, Peacock, Apple TV+, Canal+ and ADN, among others.

## What your friends will see

A streaming presence fills the Rich Presence card with what is on screen. With Netflix, for example:

- **Watching Netflix** at the top.
- The title of the series or film on the first line.
- For a series, the season and episode, written like `S1.E3`, followed by the episode title. For a film, its year.
- The poster as the main image, and a play or pause badge.
- While it plays, the time elapsed and the time left.
- A button, **Watch Episode** or **Watch Movie**, that opens the same title for your friends.

Prime Video and Disney+ work the same way, with the season and episode when the player shows them. Crunchyroll shows the series, the episode title and a cover, and adds a button to the series page.

## Watching, not browsing

By default, streaming presences focus on what is in the player, so your status doesn't change every time you hesitate between two series. The details vary a little:

- **Netflix** shows nothing until a title plays: the home page, search and title pages stay private.
- **Prime Video** and **Disney+** also show the title page you are on, as **Viewing details** or **Viewing a series** with its name. Home, search and lists stay private.
- **Crunchyroll** also shows its main pages, such as a series page, the simulcast calendar, your watchlist or a search. Turn on its **Privacy mode** to hide the titles.

To show more, turn on **Show browsing activity** in the presence's settings in the side panel. Your status then follows you on the home page, in lists and in search, where it can show what you typed.

## Why this works when screen sharing doesn't

If you have tried to share your screen on Discord while watching Netflix, you have probably seen a black rectangle instead of the video. Streaming services protect their video with DRM, and browsers keep protected video out of screen captures.

Rich Presence is a different thing: it never sends the video, only text and a poster that describe it. That's why it works with every service, and why it doesn't break the rules of the platform. If you want to watch together, use the platform's own group viewing feature when it has one, and let your Discord status tell your friends what is on.

## Keep some things to yourself

Not every evening needs an audience. A few quick options:

- **Pause everything** with **Ctrl+Shift+U** (**Cmd+Shift+U** on a Mac), and press it again to resume.
- **Hide this tab** in the side panel to keep one tab private while others stay shared.
- **Snooze** a presence for an hour, four hours or until tomorrow.
- **Share only at certain hours** with a schedule, for all presences or just the streaming ones.
- On **Crunchyroll**, turn on **Privacy mode** to hide the title while still showing that you are watching.

All the details are in [Choose exactly what Discord shows about you](/guides/control-what-discord-shows).

## If your status stays empty

- Make sure the title is actually playing in the browser, not in a TV app or on your phone.
- Check that you are on the address the presence supports: Prime Video works on `primevideo.com`, Netflix on `netflix.com`.
- Reload the tab once after installing a presence.

For everything else, the [troubleshooting checklist](/guides/rich-presence-not-showing) goes through each link of the chain.
