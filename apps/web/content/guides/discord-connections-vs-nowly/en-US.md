Discord can show some activities on its own. Link your Spotify account in Discord's settings, and your profile says **Listening to Spotify** without installing anything else. So why does the Nowly library have a Spotify presence at all? Because the two approaches work in very different ways, and each one is better in some situations. This guide compares them.

## How Discord connections work

In Discord, **User Settings** then **Connections** lets you link accounts from other services. For a few of them, the link also feeds your activity:

- **Spotify**: once linked, Discord shows the track you are playing. Spotify reports it to Discord from its own servers, so it works whether you listen on your phone, a speaker or your computer. Friends can also listen along, which requires Spotify Premium.
- **Crunchyroll**: a linked account lets Discord show what you watch on Crunchyroll.
- **Twitch** and **YouTube**: these connections are mostly about your own channel, for example showing that you are live and syncing subscriber roles in servers. They do not show the videos or streams you watch.

The list of services and what each one shares change over time, so check the **Connections** page in your Discord settings for the current state.

## How Nowly works

Nowly does not link any account. The presence for a website reads what is playing in your browser tab, and the desktop app hands it to the Discord app on the same computer. Nothing is exchanged between the platform and Discord. The [library](/library) marks platforms that Discord already supports through a connection with an **Already on Discord** notice, so you know a choice exists.

## The differences that matter

| | Discord connection | Nowly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | Link your account once | Extension, desktop app and a presence |
| Where it works | Any device the platform reports from | Your computer's browser |
| Account link | Discord and the platform share data | No link between them |
| What is shown | Decided by the integration | Decided by the presence, with your settings |
| Privacy options | Discord's activity settings | Pause, schedules, privacy mode, hidden tabs |
| Platforms | A handful | More than 40, growing |

## When a Discord connection is the better choice

- You mostly listen on your phone or a speaker, not in a browser.
- You want friends to be able to listen along on Spotify.
- You don't want to install anything on your computer.

In that case, use the connection and don't install the matching Nowly presence.

## When Nowly is the better choice

- You would rather not link your Discord and streaming accounts together.
- You want finer control: hide the title with a privacy mode, share only in the evening with a schedule, or pause everything with **Ctrl+Shift+U**.
- You use the web version of the platform, and you also want activity from platforms Discord doesn't support, like Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Twitch viewing or ChatGPT, all managed in one place.

## Don't use both for the same platform

If both the Spotify connection and the Nowly Spotify presence are active, Discord receives the same song twice, from two sources. Depending on the moment, your profile can show two activities, switch between them, or show one that is out of date. Choose one per platform:

- either turn off **Display Spotify as your status** in the Spotify connection settings (the wording may vary), or unlink it,
- or remove or turn off the Nowly presence for that platform.

## A practical mix

Many people combine both: the Spotify connection for music, because they listen on their phone all day, and Nowly for everything they watch in the browser. As long as each platform has a single source, Discord shows a clean, up-to-date status.
