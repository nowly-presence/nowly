# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Nowly community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other personal characteristic.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information about others without explicit permission.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional in a community or professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, community channels, and project-related events. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

Project maintainers may clarify or enforce this Code of Conduct when behavior occurs outside project spaces and has a material impact on the safety or participation of the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at [contact@nowly.me](mailto:contact@nowly.me). For private GitHub reports, use [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/nowly-presence/nowly/security/advisories/new) only for security vulnerabilities; ordinary conduct reports should be sent by email.

Reports should include:

- What happened and when.
- Where it happened, including links or message references when available.
- The people involved or affected.
- Any relevant screenshots or other evidence.
- Whether you want your identity kept confidential.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and confidentially as practical. They may remove or edit harmful content, warn a participant, temporarily or permanently restrict participation, or take any other action necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled respectfully and will not be publicly discussed without the reporter's permission, except where disclosure is required for safety or legal reasons.

## False Reports and Retaliation

Reports made in good faith are welcome, even when the reported behavior is ultimately found not to violate this Code of Conduct. Knowingly false reports, retaliation against someone who reports or participates in an investigation, and attempts to evade an enforcement decision are themselves unacceptable behavior.
