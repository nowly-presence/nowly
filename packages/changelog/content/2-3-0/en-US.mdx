## What's new

- Optional sign-in with Discord, in Settings under "Account and sync". Without an account, Nowly works exactly as before.
- Once signed in, your installed presences (whether they are on, and their schedules), your settings and each presence's settings follow you to your other browsers. Presences received from another browser install themselves through the usual signed download.
- On the first sign-in, if the account and the browser both hold data, Nowly asks which one to keep.
- Your current activity, tabs, visited pages and history are never synced. Developer settings and the custom API address stay on each device.
- "Sign out" keeps everything on the device. "Stop syncing and erase the online copy" erases the synced copy and signs out all your devices.
- nowly.me/account lists your linked devices and lets you download your data or delete your account.
- New seasonal theme variations: a week of spring, summer, autumn and winter around each solstice and equinox, plus the new year, each with its own colours and Nolo dressed for the occasion. On the day itself Nolo drops by with a little greeting, and on January 1 he counts down to the new year with fireworks.
- The extension's theme is now connected to nowly.me: with seasonal themes on, the site wears the same season as your extension. Turn seasonal themes off in Settings and both go back to normal.

## Improvements

- Nolo now introduces new features once, the first time you open a new version.
- Likes count once per account when several of your devices are linked.
- When Discord is started as administrator and blocks Nowly, a message explains how to restore the connection.
- You can now support Nowly from Settings, under "About", with Ko-fi or GitHub Sponsors. After 14 days of use, a small card at the bottom of the Activity tab may also suggest it: "Later" hides it for 90 days, and it never shows during an activity. Everything stays on your device.
- nowly.me/support now has a donation card with Ko-fi and GitHub Sponsors.
