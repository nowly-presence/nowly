## What's new

- A new Seasonal theme in Settings > Appearance & language: it follows your system's light or dark mode and adds a seasonal theme when one is running. It is on by default and turns off as soon as you pick System, Light or Dark. If you had chosen Light or Dark, your choice is kept.
- All through October, Nowly gets ready for Halloween: an orange palette in both light and dark mode, and a few quiet touches in empty screens.
- On October 31, Nolo, our mascot, has a little surprise for you the first time you open the panel. It stays gentle, and calmer still when your system asks for reduced motion.

## Improvements

- Hide the supported site suggestion for one presence with "Don't show again". Hidden suggestions can be brought back from Settings.
