## Creator

- The new Create tab lets you build your own Discord presence for any website without writing code: pick the pages, what they show and up to two buttons per page, then check the preview.
- Your drafts sync between your devices and can be imported or exported as a JSON file.
- Send your presence for review and follow its status, with the team's feedback field by field. Edit and resend it until it is published.
- Your presences are automatically translated into every Nowly language.
- In the Library, your creations are grouped by status and can be edited from their page.

## Reports

- Reporting a presence now opens a conversation with the Nowly team, found in the Activity tab under "Open conversations". The team can reply, ask for details with answer choices and update the status, and you can answer, add up to two images and close the conversation.
- You choose to report anonymously or with your Discord pseudo, and whether to be notified of replies. Reporting from the website opens the same conversation.
- A conversation without a reply closes on its own after 14 days.

## Account and website

- An account space lets you manage your public profile, your devices, your presences and the progress of your creations.
- Your public profile shows your links (five at most), a "verified" badge and your presences as cards, like in the Library.
- The Discord author of created presences appears with their avatar.

## Extension

- New "Text size" setting (Normal, Medium, Large), set to Medium by default and introduced by Nolo after the Create tab tour.
- The navigation bar now shows as two bubbles by default.
- Announcements are automatically translated into every language.
- The toolbar badge setting is removed, along with the privacy note under "About".
- The "Stop syncing" button is shorter and the sync hint is trimmed.
