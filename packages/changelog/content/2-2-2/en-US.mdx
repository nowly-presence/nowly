## Fixes

- Repeated native heartbeat responses no longer fill Runtime logs. Nowly records a heartbeat when the connection state, status, or native version changes, while keeping errors visible.
- Very short Discord asset tooltips are ignored before an activity is sent, avoiding invalid RPC payloads.
