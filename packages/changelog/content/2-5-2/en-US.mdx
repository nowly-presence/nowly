## Fixes

- Fixed the extension sometimes staying stuck on "Unable to save your confirmation, or the documents have changed" after being reopened: "Review documents" now checks the documents again right away instead of waiting up to 5 minutes, and a failed background check no longer blocks the extension when the documents are already known.
- Added a "Close" button next to "Review documents" on that screen.
