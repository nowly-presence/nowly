## What's new

- Custom activities: a new Custom tab to show an activity you write yourself on Discord, with no presence to install. Set a name, a type (Playing, Listening, Watching, Competing), two lines of text, a large and a small image with hover text, time elapsed or a countdown, and up to two buttons.
- Save as many custom activities as you like and turn one on with a switch. While it is on, it replaces the activity from your tabs.
- Images your way: upload a file (resized automatically, 1 MB max), paste an https link, pick a coloured icon from 24, or keep the Nowly logo. Uploaded images stay on your device and are sent again automatically if they expired after 30 days without use.
- Ten templates to start from (Studying, Coding, Game night, Playlist, Movie night, Live, Workout, Travelling, Coffee break, Focus).
- A live preview in the editor that matches what Discord shows.
- A customizable navigation bar: hold a tab to move it, or pull it out to either side to create another bar. Reset it anytime from Settings.

## Improvements

- Activity cards now follow Discord's layout for each type: "Playing" shows the name on its own line, Listening, Watching and Competing put it next to the verb.
- Dialogs now float above the panel instead of sticking to its bottom edge.
- The active tab label is no longer cut off.
